Our Story

A quarter century of quiet obsession with the perfect plate.

The Belle Époque Spirit

La Belle Époque opened its doors in 1998 in a narrow stone townhouse on Rue Saint-Honoré. Chef Julien Moreau, fresh from kitchens in Lyon and Modena, arrived with a simple idea: that a meal should feel like a memory being made.

Today, twenty-five years later, that idea still guides every dish. Our menus are composed seasonally — driven by what arrives at the market each morning, not by what's convenient. Our kitchen is quiet and deliberate. Our dining room, candlelit and warm.

We believe in slow food, honest ingredients, and the kind of hospitality that feels less like service and more like being welcomed into someone's home.

— Julien Moreau, Chef & Founder
