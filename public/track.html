Our Story

A quarter century of quiet obsession with the perfect plate — now in the heart of Nairobi.

Nairobi's Belle Époque

La Belle Époque opened its doors in 1998 in Paris, and since 2024 has called Kimathi Street in Nairobi's CBD home. Chef Julien Moreau and his Kenyan team bring together French technique and East African produce — a menu that feels both timeless and unmistakably local.

Today, we source from small farms in Kiambu, Limuru and Nyeri. Our beef is grass-fed from Laikipia. Our coffee — roasted a few blocks away. Every dish tells a story of two continents meeting on a plate.

We believe in slow food, honest ingredients, and the kind of hospitality that feels less like service and more like being welcomed into someone's home.

— Julien Moreau, Chef & Founder
